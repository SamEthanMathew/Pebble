What Pebble does for you

Real planners doing real reasoning — not a chatbot pretending to.

☀

Morning briefing

One summary every morning combining today's calendar, unread email triage, and upcoming deadlines.

✉

Comms triage

Classifies your inbox into action-required / FYI / ignore, and drafts replies for the messages that need one.

⏱

Smart scheduling

Reasons about how your day is allocated — not just lists events, but flags conflicts and suggests free-window use.

📚

Exam-prep planning

Give Pebble a course and an exam date; it reasons backward to produce a day-by-day study plan you can run.

🌙

Daily wrap-up

An evening reflection plus a preview of tomorrow, appended to your Obsidian daily note.

🗂

Vault-backed memory

Pebble's persistent knowledge lives in your Obsidian vault, with provenance markers so you always see what's yours vs. Pebble's.

🧠

Thinking passes

Scheduled reasoning over your own notes — connect, drift, emerge, ideas, challenge, ghost — never over Pebble's own writeback.

Safe by default

Every outbound action passes through an autonomy layer.

Tier-gated actions

Every action is one of AUTO, NOTIFY, or ASK. Send-email actions are ASK. Reading the calendar is AUTO. You stay in control of what runs without asking you.

First-time approval

The first invocation of any action — and the first message to any new recipient — forces an approval popup even if its declared tier is AUTO. No surprises.

Dry-run during onboarding

The setup wizard turns dry-run on by default. Every outbound API call is logged as a preview file so you can read what Pebble would have done before you trust it to send.

Provenance on every write

Notes Pebble writes to your vault carry a source: pebble marker. Pebble's chokepoint refuses to overwrite content you authored — your notes stay yours.

Local-first by default

You can run Pebble entirely on a local Ollama model with no cloud calls. Cloud planners (Claude, GPT, Gemini) are opt-in via the setup wizard.

Full audit trail

Every action Pebble takes is appended to ~/.pebble/audit.jsonl. Type /how-am-i-doing in chat for a summary, or /audit for the raw rows.
